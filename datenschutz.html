Privacy Policy – Letterherz Pin Publisher

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

Letterherz is a small online shop for vintage typography gifts, run by Steffen Spohr, Amselweg 54, 71116 Gärtringen, Germany. Contact: steffen.spohr@icloud.com. Shop: letterherz.etsy.com

2. What the app does

"Letterherz Pin Publisher" is a private, first-party tool. It is used only by the owner of the Letterherz Pinterest business account to publish Pins of Letterherz's own product photos to Letterherz's own boards, each Pin linking to the matching product page in the Letterherz Etsy shop. The app is not offered to other Pinterest users and has no public sign-up.

3. Which data the app accesses

  • Only data of the Letterherz Pinterest account itself: board names and IDs, Pins created by the app, and basic account information needed to publish.
  • Requested scopes: boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write, user_accounts:read.
  • The app does not access, collect or store data of any other Pinterest user, follower or visitor.

4. Authentication

The app connects to Pinterest exclusively through Pinterest's official OAuth 2.0 flow. No Pinterest passwords or session cookies are ever requested or stored. The OAuth access token is stored encrypted by our integration provider (AnythingMCP) and is used only for the publishing actions described above.

5. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent or share any Pinterest data with third parties. Data is not used for advertising, profiling or training of any kind.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep only the OAuth token and a list of the Pins we published (Pin ID, board, linked product). Access can be revoked at any time in the Pinterest account settings. On revocation or on request by email, all stored tokens and records are deleted within 30 days.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Contact: steffen.spohr@icloud.com.

8. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). It uses no cookies, no tracking and no analytics. GitHub may log technical access data (such as IP addresses) for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
